Customer Support FAQ

1. Order Tracking
Customers can track their orders by providing their order ID.
The order ID format is ORD followed by digits.

2. Delivery
Orders marked as Shipped are on their way to the customer.
Orders marked as Processing are being prepared.
Estimated delivery dates may change.

3. Refund Policy
Delivered orders are eligible to request a refund within 7 days of delivery.
Refund eligibility does not mean the refund has already been issued.
Orders that have not been delivered may require a support agent to check cancellation options.

4. Complaints
Customers can report damaged or incorrect items.
A support ticket is created when a complaint is registered.
The ticket initially has an Open status.

5. Human Support
Customers can request human support if their issue cannot be resolved by the automated assistant.
